Types of Spectacle Lenses

The right lens for your eyes.

Early eye care and the right lens design prevent vision deterioration and keep you comfortable, whatever your day demands.

01

Single Vision

Used to correct both myopia (short-sightedness) and hyperopia (far-sightedness). These can also provide reading corrections for presbyopia.

02

Accommodative Lenses

Designed to address focus strain, particularly for close-range work. They blend like multifocal lenses, with distance up top and a softer graduation to near.

03

Bifocals

Spectacle lenses with two distinct optical powers, where the bottom segment corrects the near vision associated with presbyopia.

04

Myopia Control LensesHoya MYOSMART

We use MYOSMART technology from Hoya Optics, which employs a ring-shaped treatment area to slow myopia progression while maintaining clear vision.

05

Multifocal (Progressive) Lenses

Enable clear vision at near, intermediate and far distances without switching between glasses — ideal for reading, computer work and driving.
