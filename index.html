About Us

Great products, impeccable service — every visit.

Welcome to Jason Stander Optometrists, conveniently located at the Bendor Cycad Centre in Polokwane. We provide tailored eye care using state-of-the-art technology and equipment, ensuring you feel at ease and receive the best in vision health.

Whether you're after Tom Ford, Gucci, Silhouette, Lindberg, Mykita, Oakley, Versace, Fendi, Ray-Ban or something a little more low-key — our comprehensive frame selection spans affordable everyday options to designer eyewear, fitted correctly for your face and your life.

Our Services

Eye care built around you.

General Eye Exam

Assesses vision and eye health, checking for refractive errors, disease, and overall well-being of the eyes.

Eye Disease Diagnosis

Comprehensive testing to identify conditions like glaucoma, cataracts or diabetic retinopathy, ensuring timely treatment.

Contact Lens Fitting

Custom-fitted contact lenses to correct vision, offering comfort and convenience for daily wear and activities.

Driver's License Certification

We evaluate vision requirements to ensure you meet the legal standards for safe driving.
